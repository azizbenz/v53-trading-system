# V5.3 Final Gap / Decision / Calibration Register

## 1. Register Rules

- This register is cumulative.
- After each audit-relevant correction, semantic patch, evidence pack, or human decision, this register must be updated in the same workstream or immediately after it.
- Open gaps remain visible until resolved.
- Resolved gaps move to `RESOLVED_GAPS` with evidence and date.
- Future calibrations remain open until data-backed artifacts exist.
- A gap is not resolved by intention; it is resolved only by patch, report, or test evidence.
- `NOT_BLOCKING` does not mean `RESOLVED`.
- `FUTURE_RESERVED` does not mean forgotten.
- Every inserted or changed row must carry an explicit date in its own tracking column: `Last update`, `Resolved date`, or `Date`. If the exact date cannot be proven from evidence, use `UNKNOWN_DATE`.
- This register is the working reference contract for remaining work; historical items are never deleted, only reclassified with evidence.
- If the exact resolution date cannot be proven from the evidence, use `UNKNOWN_DATE`.
