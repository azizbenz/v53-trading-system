Stage 1 - Training identity and scientific controls

Lock the pair, timeframe, feature set, and core training hyperparameters before launching the bounded ML rail.

Stage 2 - Historical training and audit sources

Klines are consumed by the current expected-edge rail. Orderbook, trades, and metadata are surfaced for audit traceability and future execution-grounded ML slices.

Stage 3 - Windows and governed launch

Refresh source-scope proof first when roots or dates change, then launch only when the rail remains scientifically identifiable.
